Have you ever felt the need to become the very music you want to put out? I understand this feeling since I started making music just over 9 years ago.

I have been making music for the last few years and over this time, it has been very on and off.

I have enjoyed it truly as a hobby. The feeling of exporting a newly made song, excited to create a next song or going over music video ideas in my head.

The thing is, I feel stumped by own thinking at times and what exactly I want from music. It has not always been so clear.

Wohno (my artist name) does not necessarily want to become famous and sign a record deal. Love for the art and passion for the craft is enough to keep me coming back for more.

Now jump to 2025 and Playboi Carti's "Music" album has released. Hosted by DJ Swamp Izzo. An influential South Carolinian native described by some as 'Rap's Best Kept Secret' because of his ongoing impact. [Everybodywiki](https://en.everybodywiki.com/Swamp_Izzo)

This album sparked something within me. The SFX, the over-the-top vocal chants, the skilful mixing on these songs, made me come to a realisation.

How much did I want to actually become the music I AM creating. Other human beings are already doing the same thing. Why couldn’t I take part in this opportunity?

I took the chance and will continue to do so. I hope you also do the same.

Speak soon.
